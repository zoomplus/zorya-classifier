# H5 draft: grouped nomination from text-grounded and UI proposals

**State: accepted development plan; H5a native generation is closed and H5b selection is being implemented.** This document fixes the hypothesis; phase-specific reports record execution. No accuracy claim or public promotion follows from the plan. H4f remains skipped. H4e's independent UI evaluation reports candidate availability on all 3 positive controls; that remains a one-screenshot candidate-availability result, not a selector result.

## Question and evidence

Can two complementary proposal families retain the H3b photo candidate ceiling, provide H4e-like UI candidates, and create useful map candidates without choosing a family from the benchmark's photo/UI/map labels?

H3b's text-conditioned GroundingDINO → SAM2 recipe produced correct positive localizations on 3/3 photos, 0/3 UI cases and 0/3 map cases, and false completed outputs on all three verified negatives. H4e's fixed 25%-per-side prompt expansion of UI-detector boxes before SAM2 produced an independently evaluated valid mask-derived candidate for 3/3 positives on its controlled Geany screenshot. These measurements motivate a proposal-composition test; they do not show that either generator is a general-purpose detector or that a union will improve semantic selection. H3c's mask-aware criteria motivate the final acceptance rule: identity, mask purity, and whole visible extent must all hold for a located result.

The papers support only the complementary roles being tested. Grounding DINO uses category names and referring expressions for open-set boxes; it can still miss a target. OmniParser's detector is trained to find interactable UI regions, not arbitrary photo objects or game-map units. SAM 2 accepts prompts for segmentation but does not certify that a mask is the requested instance. Set-of-Mark work motivates stable, human-readable marks for candidate views; it does not establish that a model can correctly rank a large candidate pool. See [Grounding DINO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.05499), [OmniParser](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00203), [SAM 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00714), and [Set-of-Mark prompting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11441).

## Recommended hypothesis: run both families on every image, nominate within family

Run both explicitly staged proposal routes for every input, without reading strata, case IDs, evaluator labels, or source manifests to route work:

1. **Text-grounded family (`D00`–`D35`).** Reuse the frozen H3b Grounding DINO threshold/text recipe and native order, then use SAM 2 with each original Grounding DINO box. Preserve the text, box, detector score, and mask as provenance; scores remain diagnostics.
2. **UI family (`U00`–`U35`).** Run the frozen OmniParser icon detector and keep its existing stable detector/NMS order, then use SAM 2 with the H4e fixed 25%-per-side expanded input box. Keep both original and expanded boxes. Do not clip the output mask to either box.
3. **Independent family nominations.** Present each family's actual mask candidates in its own closed `DecisionPort` Choice with `rankingSize: 4` and `none`. Retain the returned ordered ranking, raw categorical scores/metadata, and stable input-order ties. If `none` appears in the top four, it occupies that rank but is not a candidate nominee; an empty family remains an empty nomination. Never compare scores between Grounding DINO and OmniParser, between their SAM 2 masks, or across the two nomination calls.
4. **One final choice over at most eight actual nominees.** Show the unmodified full source and the nominated masks with clean context, actual overlays, and masked cutouts. A single closed final Choice over the nominated aliases plus `none` accepts a candidate only when its mask is the authored target instance, excludes substantial unrelated foreground/background, and covers the whole visible instance within the ROI. `none`, ambiguity, invalid output, or a failed requirement yields unknown/non-location; a categorical tie resolves to the stable first option in input order and alone does not force unknown. None does not assert global absence. This final request combines identity, purity, and extent, so it replaces a separate post-selection box-check call for this H5 profile.

Family aliases preserve identity and order without score mixing. The final stage receives candidates only from the two fixed family rankings; it cannot ask either proposal generator to add candidates or invent coordinates. Present the source plus one large review card per nominee (at most eight), with `clean`, `overlay`, and `cutout` views for that nominee and labels outside image pixels. That is at most nine image parts; do not compress eight nominees into two small contact sheets. Preserve all 72-or-fewer source masks for evaluator-only candidate-availability analysis, independently of the up-to-eight candidates nominated for final choice. A truth-best candidate is never substituted for a family nominee. For final categorical ties, take the raw maximum with stable input-order tie resolution; a tie alone never forces `unknown`. Return `unknown` only for an actual invalid/ambiguous/unresolved semantic outcome or failure to satisfy the explicit criteria.

### Bounded work and adapter fit

Per source, the hard maximum is two detector forwards (one DINO, one OmniParser), one SAM 2 image encode, and 72 serial SAM 2 box decodes (36 per family). Load and unload the two detector models sequentially, then load one SAM 2 model and decode the two frozen box lists serially; do not batch all 72 decoder prompts. Retain packed source masks on CPU, with a 72-candidate cap and a 19 MiB aggregate packed-mask ceiling. This accommodates 72 masks at the existing 2,097,152-pixel source ceiling (18,874,368 bytes before small metadata), while preserving the existing source pixel bound. Any exceeded budget is an explicit technical failure; do not silently drop masks or reduce a family based on its score.

The current grounded-mask selector accepts at most 36 candidates, groups six per contact sheet, and labels `C00`–`C35`. The current entity limits allow 9 image parts per `DecisionRequest`, 16 MiB per image, and 32 MiB for the serialized body. H5 therefore needs a **benchmark-only** grouped selector extension: keep each family nomination at no more than 36 candidates, retaining the existing 6-per-sheet layout and its maximum of 7 image parts (one source plus six sheets). The final stage uses one source plus up to eight separate nominee cards, at most nine image parts. Across the three requests the maximum is 7 + 7 + 9 = 23 image parts; each request independently obeys the nine-image, 16 MiB-per-image and 32 MiB serialized-body caps. Set a fixed aggregate presentation-byte ceiling and fail before dispatch if PNG/base64 serialization would exceed the 32 MiB adapter limit. The final request has one source and up to eight large nominee cards (nine image parts maximum); each card contains labeled clean, overlay, and cutout panels at readable resolution. Do not raise public API limits or candidate aliases in this research screen. For a family with fewer than four candidates, request `rankingSize: 4`; the validated Choice selector returns the available `min(4, keyCount)` ranked aliases. Preserve exactly those returned aliases and their raw distribution. For ranked-only responses, retain the provided order and do not invent probability values. For full categorical distributions, rank by raw score descending with stable input-order ties, then keep at most four. Do not treat a tie as an automatic `unknown`.

Native H5a is a distinct, producer-only phase. Replay the exact frozen H3b DINO/SAM2 outputs and hashes; generate the OmniParser plus 25%-expanded SAM2 family for all twelve cases, caching only identical source SHA-256 values (the four UI prompts share one screenshot). Do not access strata or truth to route sources. Run one owned GPU worker; cap each unique image at 36 serial SAM2 box decodes, 240 seconds per image, 2,097,152 source pixels, and 19 MiB aggregate packed masks per case. Stream hashes while writing; record input, model, runtime, and producer hashes both before and after generation. If numerical output differs from prior H4e on the shared screenshot, record the runtime/scheduling difference; do not rerun H4e to force equivalence. No paid calls occur in H5a.

Any later composition replay is a separately frozen experiment. Its planned maximum is three `DecisionPort` calls per case (two family rankings and one final Choice), 36 physical requests over the fixed 12-case pilot, with fallbacks/retries disabled and physical attempts recorded at fetch. Native-generation wall time and later paid-replay latency must be reported separately; do not claim a 240-second end-to-end deadline until the composition exists. Reuse only already staged caller-owned H3b DINO, OmniParser, and SAM 2 assets; no asset acquisition is part of H5a.

### Alternatives considered

| Design                                      | Native work per source                                          | Decision work                                                                     | Image-parts ceiling                                         | Main trade-off                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Union all 72 in one ranking                 | Both detectors; up to 72 serial SAM 2 decodes                   | One 72-choice selection plus separate verification (2 calls)                      | 9 exactly: source plus 8 redesigned 3×3 sheets              | Keeps every proposal in one ranking but requires a 72-alias selector/layout change, fills the request image limit, increases visual/choice clutter, and risks exceeding the 32 MiB body limit.              |
| Closed image-kind router                    | Router selects one family; one detector; up to 36 SAM 2 decodes | Route + selection + verification (3 calls)                                        | At most 7 parts/request                                     | Lower native cost, but can route a UI-like object to DINO or a natural/map target to Omni; adds an image classifier decision and can leak strata if any benchmark metadata or heuristic chooses the family. |
| **Grouped family nomination (recommended)** | Both detectors; up to 72 serial SAM 2 decodes                   | Two within-family top-four nominations plus one mask-aware final Choice (3 calls) | At most 7 parts for a family request and 9 for final choice | Keeps each model's ranking local and exposes a small final set with readable nominee cards; spends one extra call over the single-union selector and can lose the target if its family ranking omits it.    |

The router has a lower native ceiling but risks the exact missed-family behavior H5 is meant to measure. The one-shot union is simpler semantically but uses all image slots and asks one request to discriminate among 72 masks. Grouped nomination is the cleanest test of complementarity while retaining the current selector's family-sized request shape. Its family shortlist recall must be reported, not assumed.

## Frozen evaluation and stop rule

Use the same fixed 12-case DEV panel (all four photos, four UI cases, and four map cases) for which H3b is already closed, including all three verified negatives. H5a freezes both source and case hashes, authored target/exclusion text, H3b producer/run/mask hashes, Omni detector settings/order, H4e padding transform, SAM 2 revision/assets/runtime, producer code, native memory/pixel/mask caps, and generation deadline before inference. A later composition freeze separately pins renderer, request text, caller-owned `DecisionPort` recipe, paid-call/cost budgets, evaluator, and its own latency deadline. The native producer runs both families for every case row; source-byte-identical Omni/SAM2 outputs may be content-hash reused. No routing may use strata, case ID, filenames, target-class heuristics, or evaluator data. Only the independent evaluator joins truth after native masks and receipts are sealed.

H5a first reports each family's and the union's candidate availability separately under the unchanged same-candidate gate (bounds IoU at least 0.50 and target coverage at least 0.90), retaining all rows, truncation counts, errors, and negatives. Development aims are at least 3/3 photo positives available, 3/3 UI positives available, and 2/3 map positives available; these are candidate ceilings, not selections. Report generation latency, peak RAM/VRAM, and packed-mask bytes separately. Only a later composition replay reports final selected-localization counts separately by stratum, false locations on all three negatives, correct abstentions/unknowns, mask-purity and box-completeness outcomes, nomination ranks, its own latency, physical calls, known cost, and unknown cost. Do not let a high candidate ceiling conceal a wrong or contaminated final choice.

The profile is a candidate for a later full DEV40 plan only if it produces zero false locations on the three negatives, preserves at least the H3b 3/9 positive localization count with the photo 3/3 retained, and improves UI/map positive localization without increased technical failure. If either family repeatedly adds only distractors, if the true candidate is commonly cut by the family top-four, or if limits fail, stop and preserve the run; do not enlarge the ranking, adjust thresholds, use oracle-best candidates, or add a route classifier after seeing outcomes. H5 is not package/API promotion or heldout qualification.
