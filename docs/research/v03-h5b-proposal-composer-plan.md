# H5b benchmark-only proposal composer

**State: implementation plan frozen before code.** This is a development mechanism test only. It does not select a detector family from case metadata, add a public export, promote a profile, or authorize provider dispatch.

## Question and fixed mechanism

Test whether independently nominated candidates from the frozen Grounding DINO/SAM 2 and OmniParser/SAM 2 proposal families can be combined into a useful final set without routing by image type or mixing scores from different calls. Grounding DINO supports open-set text-conditioned proposals, OmniParser targets UI elements, SAM 2 produces promptable masks without certifying semantic identity, and Set-of-Mark work motivates stable visible aliases. These sources do not guarantee candidate recall or accurate selection. [Grounding DINO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.05499), [OmniParser](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00203), [SAM 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00714), [Set-of-Mark prompting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11441).

For every source image, accept two caller-supplied ordered candidate families: DINO (`D00`–`D35`) and UI (`U00`–`U35`). Each family has at most 36 candidates. A nomination `DecisionPort` call is made for each non-empty family, using a closed `rankingSize: 4` Choice over that family's aliases plus `none`. A rank-only answer stays rank-only. A native categorical distribution is retained raw and ordered by descending supplied score with stable input-order ties. `none` consumes one of the top four ranks but is not a nominee. No score is compared across calls or families.

The final call, when at least one nominee exists, presents the unmodified source and one large clean-context/mask-overlay/masked-cutout card for each of at most eight nominees, then makes a closed `rankingSize: 1` Choice over those aliases plus `none`. Only the selected nominee can yield a location, and only from its actual mask foreground and source-space bounds. `none` or any non-complete/ambiguous outcome yields unknown, not global absence. For tied native maximum scores, stable final criterion order selects the first alias; ties do not force unknown. Criteria repeat the exact authored target and exclusions and require same-instance identity, mask purity, and whole-visible-instance completeness. They must judge the actual requested object and major unrelated regions, not demand pixel-perfect segmentation: ordinary minor contour deviations, antialiasing, and small interior holes alone are acceptable when the visible instance remains identifiable and substantially complete. Judge visible source pixels only: an occluder can correctly create a gap, and separated visible pieces can belong to one instance. Do not infer or require pixels hidden behind occlusion. Material omissions of visible target content or substantial unrelated foreground/background remain disqualifying. No model-generated coordinates are accepted.

### H3c presentation diagnosis and H5 criterion clarification (pre-freeze amendment)

After the H3c run closed, a capture-only reconstruction replayed its exact photo-02 input through the frozen H3c verifier and production `imageState`, with a local stub that returned `unknown`. The source bytes matched SHA-256 `ad0eb4bf4a6c2c1be585846a943aa06ec2440696b7e8874374904eb25dcc594a`; the selected C00 mask was the frozen H3b mask (`1680deae5eda207cc9b5958dd2fb08864e7987fd8065284a754e2a2909654db3`). The four prepared PNGs were pixel-identical to their four verifier inputs: unmodified source 1024×683, and clean context, mask overlay, and masked cutout 684×447 each. Thus this reconstruction found no resize, padding, source/mask correspondence, color, or alpha defect in the H3c request. It is not evidence that every other H3c case rendered correctly.

Visually, photo-02's bird is readily identifiable and most of its visible extent is present. The dark branch crossing the bird is an occluder in the source, so the foreground gap at that location is appropriate; the detached lower-right piece appears to be a separated visible part of the bird rather than evidence of another object. H3c's `match-incomplete` answer therefore raises a plausible over-strictness question, but does not prove the verifier was wrong. The diagnosis is recorded separately in [`v03-h3c-photo-02-render-capture-diagnosis.md`](v03-h3c-photo-02-render-capture-diagnosis.md), with the local capture manifest and pixel-correspondence hashes for reproduction.

For H5, final selection is a relative comparison among up to eight actual nominees, unlike H3c's absolute verification of one selected mask. Preserve an explicit `none` for a set with no reliable target candidate and use no universal confidence cutoff. The H5 request should tolerate ordinary minor segmentation edge errors, correctly occluded gaps, and disconnected visible components while requiring the requested object's whole _visible_ extent and rejecting major contamination or missing visible portions. The evaluator's frozen quantitative candidate-availability/localization gates, mask IoU, target coverage, and box IoU remain unchanged; this language does not relax those gates or authorize per-case prompt/threshold changes. This amendment was added before final H5 prompt criteria are frozen; no H5 provider request has been dispatched.

The criteria order is D nominees in nomination rank order, then U nominees in nomination rank order, then `none`. Family aliases retain their family prefix and original candidate identity. The producer runs both proposal families for every image and never receives case IDs, strata, labels, or evaluator truth. The evaluator may inspect all proposals independently, but it cannot alter nominations or final candidates.

## Resource, timing, and ownership bounds

The module accepts caller-owned ports for decision, image preparation, and budget/physical receipt recording. It is benchmark-only and stays outside package exports. It owns and snapshots source bytes, packed masks, aliases, target text, exclusions, candidate order, geometry, and signals before its first await. It validates no more than 36 masks per family, 72 total, and a 19 MiB aggregate packed-mask ceiling before retaining inputs. Masks stay packed across family nomination; decode one at a time for presentation, clear decoded pixels and rendered buffers when each request settles, and retain only the final nominees' packed masks until the final decision has settled.

Each case has one absolute 240-second deadline and at most three logical decision operations: D nomination, U nomination, final selection. Maximum physical calls are 36 over the fixed 12-case cohort. The caller supplies the budgeted/recording `DecisionPort`; H5b adds no retry or fallback. Nomination requests contain at most one source image plus six grouped sheets (seven image parts). The final request contains one source image plus at most eight nominee cards (nine parts). Enforce at most nine prepared images, at most 16 MiB per image, a fixed 32 MiB aggregate serialized-request ceiling, and the existing 32 MiB adapter request bound before dispatch. Any exceeded cap is a typed error; never truncate candidates to fit.

The full source remains at its original dimensions. Nomination cards use two 144×232 panels in a 320×284 card; final nominee cards use three 288×260 panels in a 900×320 card. Each clean, overlay, and cutout view is built at the exact native crop dimensions and then resized independently with the same `fit: contain`, nearest-neighbor transform and explicit dark letterbox background. The binary mask is applied before resizing so panel padding cannot shift foreground correspondence. These are compact review cards, not full-resolution crops; only the separate source image provides full-image detail. Portrait and landscape pixel-level checks compare each final panel to that exact transform and guard against the prior letterbox-to-mask mapping error. The test also reproduces the former letterbox-blind mask transform and asserts that its overlay differs from the pixel-correct result, retaining a deterministic failing-before witness.

Family nomination for an empty family is omitted. If both families yield no nominees, return unknown without inventing candidates. On abort, cancel and join admitted decision/render work before releasing owned inputs or returning; after settlement, an aborted request can never return a successful location. Candidate masks are validated against source dimensions and binary packing; returned boxes are derived only from their actual foreground. The final result includes exact family/rank provenance, raw Choice or ranking evidence, metadata, selected mask, and derived box.

## Verification before any provider work

Use synthetic caller-owned masks and controlled decision adapters only. Cover both-family participation, small pools, `none` occupying a top-four rank, rank-only results without invented probabilities, raw categorical ties with stable ordering, final stable maximum ties, exact target and purity/completeness language, full-source plus nominee-card image-part bounds, packed-mask and request-byte limits, input mutation after invocation, cancellation/join/no late success, caller-budget rejection, and the real Decisions adapter through a mock fetch with explicit provider and fallbacks disabled. No native inference, asset acquisition, credentials, or paid request occurs in this implementation step.

H5b does not alter the frozen H3b selector or H3c verifier, does not run the producer on the DEV corpus, and does not make an accuracy claim. A separate root-reviewed freeze is required before any evaluation dispatch.
