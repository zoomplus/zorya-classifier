# v0.3 localization evaluation design

## Objective and decision rule

The objective is a substantial, independently measured improvement in whole-object localization. It is not to accumulate attempts, pass a component probe, or promote a release by itself. Freeze the following qualification gate before acquiring or exposing qualification pixels to implementers. Do not revise its thresholds, denominators, strata, pairing, or mask policy after results exist.

The frozen qualification cohort has 120 cases: 30 positive and 10 verified-negative photographs, 30 positive and 10 verified-negative controlled application screenshots, and 30 positive and 10 verified-negative controlled map/game screenshots. A positive localization succeeds only when the returned box matches the preselected visible instance, has box IoU at least 0.50, and covers at least 0.90 of that instance's reference box. No box, `unknown`, error, timeout, malformed output, or missing/unstarted case is a miss in the positive denominator. There is no post-hoc `not applicable` bucket.

The candidate must meet all of these fixed quality gates:

| Gate                               | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Positive whole-object localization | At least 54/90 positives (60% macro and micro coincide because each stratum has 30), and at least 12/30 (40%) in **each** of photos, UI, and maps.                                                                                     |
| Paired improvement                 | At least +20 percentage points in positive whole-object success against the frozen v0.2 final-v3 recipe on identical cases; the lower endpoint of a paired, source-group-clustered 95% bootstrap interval must be strictly above zero. |
| False locations                    | At most one of 30 verified-negative cases may produce either a box or a cutout. Count a negative case once if either artifact exists; publish the separate box and cutout counts too.                                                  |
| Masks                              | At least 15/30 independently sourced, exact-canvas reference instances reach mask IoU >= 0.80. Every one of the 30 planned mask references stays in the denominator; missing, invalid, or failed predictions score zero.               |
| Completeness                       | Candidate and baseline each report all 120 planned cases. An incomplete run cannot qualify.                                                                                                                                            |

The paired baseline is the exact frozen final-v3 region-seeded producer, evaluator policy, model/provider route, and segmentation assets identified in the v0.2 evidence. It is executed as a baseline on the _new_ qualification cohort; its old prediction rows are never substituted for paired outputs. Before dispatch, verify whether the historical executable closure can be replayed byte-for-byte on the new input shape. If it cannot, define and separately name a reproducible v0.2 baseline before freezing the candidate; do not quietly change the comparator after observing scores. Both recipes receive the same per-case target and source pixels, use the same explicitly configured decision route when technically possible, and have retries/fallbacks disabled. If any route difference is unavoidable, document it in the freeze and describe the paired contrast as recipe-level, not causal.

The bootstrap is paired by case and clustered by source group, stratified by the three source strata, with 10,000 replicates and a fixed seed `0x56334551`. Resample source groups with replacement within each stratum; keep every selected group's candidate/baseline rows paired and keep all rows in the sampled group together. Compute the difference in positive-case success after giving the three strata equal weight. Publish the estimate and percentile 95% interval, along with raw counts and the sampled-group policy. A case-level bootstrap would overstate precision for repeated captures from one app or renderer and is not acceptable.

Latency and cost are mandatory secondary results, not accuracy substitutes. Capture end-to-end case latency including failures, nearest-rank p50/p95, actual physical HTTP/native dispatch counts, known cost, unknown-charge count and reserved exposure, and cost per successful positive. Run both recipes on the same host with no competing owned native workload; counterbalance candidate/baseline order within case pairs using a frozen seed. Record OS, CPU/GPU, runtime, model/provider/API, native-thread limits, warm/cold policy, and receipt hashes. Keep unknown charges in the total exposure. Compute call and dollar caps from the frozen worst-case recipe bound before any paid dispatch; stop before dispatch if the requested cohort cannot fit the explicitly authorized caps. Timing under concurrent stress is diagnostic and ineligible for ranking. Latency/cost may explain the tradeoff, but may not rescue a quality-gate failure.

## Corpus boundaries and source plan

### Existing material available to developers

The following are existing development or exposed-regression inputs. They may be used to reproduce the known failures, verify oracle/evaluator plumbing, and screen hypotheses. They cannot be relabeled as the new blind cohort:

| Purpose                           | Input path                                                                                                                   | Cases and restriction                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Licensed photo DEV                | `benchmarks/v02/corpus/dev/inputs.json`                                                                                      | 20 cases (16 positive/4 negative); use only as development. Pixels are local under ignored `benchmarks/cache/v02/corpus`.                                                                        |
| Independent controlled UI DEV     | `benchmarks/v02/corpus/dev-independent-ui/inputs.json`                                                                       | 10 cases (7/3), Geany 2.0 only; one app family.                                                                                                                                                  |
| Independent controlled map DEV    | `benchmarks/v02/corpus/dev-independent-map/inputs.json`                                                                      | 10 cases (7/3), Freeciv 3.1 only; one game/ruleset family and all positive sprites occupy one screen rectangle.                                                                                  |
| Historical controlled supplements | `benchmarks/v02/corpus/dev-controlled/inputs.json` and `benchmarks/v02/corpus/v3/`                                           | Model-assisted/historical work only; do not promote it to independent evidence.                                                                                                                  |

| Exposed 80-case v4 corpus         | `benchmarks/v02/evidence/exposed-regression-v4/inputs/{photos,ui,maps}.json`                                                 | 40 photos + 20 Mousepad + 20 Minetest cases; all exposed regression only.                                                                                                                        |
| Final v3 outcomes and evaluator   | `docs/evidence/v02/region-seeded-heldout-v3/` and `benchmarks/v02/evaluate-fresh.ts`, `benchmarks/v02/evaluate-proposals.ts` | The final v3 result has 8/60 positive whole-object boxes and 30 mask references (8 pass, 3 fail, 19 unavailable). Preserve it as historical failure evidence and an immutable baseline identity. |

Also exclude the image IDs, decoded-byte hashes, photographers/source authors, app families, game families, renderer/assets, and any exact source states already represented by earlier v0.1/v0.2 work, including cancelled or unrevealed cohorts. Derive and hash the exclusion set from existing source ledgers and source-group audits before selection. The curator may inspect exclusion metadata to prevent overlap; the implementers may not receive newly selected qualification IDs, paths, pixels, target assignments, truth, or per-case outcomes before candidate freeze. A public commitment may disclose cohort size and exclusion-ledger digest, not the hidden rows. Public training overlap cannot be ruled out; report this as a source-group-disjoint public-data qualification, not a pretraining-exclusion claim.

### New qualification cohort

Acquire no pixels until the curator has a bounded candidate shortlist. Do not download a broad dataset archive. Pin individual candidate source records first, then fetch only the bytes for admitted cases, verify their hashes, and preserve acquisition failures without substituting a visually convenient case after labels or model outputs have been seen.

**Photographs (30 positive, 10 negative).** Use a new Open Images V7 validation/test slice as the primary candidate source. Select 40 distinct image IDs and author/source groups absent from all prior source ledgers, with no duplicated decoded-image hashes or near-duplicate scenes. Positive examples require a directly corresponding official target box for one visible, unambiguous instance. Require at least 30 independently sourced exact-canvas binary-mask references across the 30 positives. First audit official COCO/LVIS instance masks for exact image identity, source dimensions, and raster transform; use a source-aligned mask only when its image-level provenance and license are verified and the stored raster is reproducible. If official masks cannot supply the frozen quota, commission independent source-pixel traces with a second annotator and adjudication under a predeclared rule. Do not count unavailable or unverified masks toward the quota. Keep the qualification mask gate at 15/30 IoU>=0.80, with every planned reference in the denominator and every missing prediction scoring zero. Negative examples require an explicit human-verified negative record for the requested target class. Missing boxes, an unknown label, an unannotated class, and absence of a prediction are never evidence of absence. The official Open Images description documents verified image-level positives/negatives, exhaustive validation/test boxes for available positive labels, and manually painted validation/test masks; its download page defines the box/mask row formats. Still retain per-image page/source, author, license URI, and license text evidence: a dataset-level annotation release does not replace image-specific rights review. [Open Images V7 description](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/factsfigures_v7.html), [Open Images V7 download and formats](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/download_v7.html). COCO publishes object segmentation annotations and dataset documentation ([COCO data](https://cocodataset.org/)); LVIS publishes instance-mask annotations over COCO images ([LVIS dataset](https://www.lvisdataset.org/), [official LVIS API](https://github.com/lvis-dataset/lvis-api)). Treat annotation and image rights separately: an LVIS annotation license does not grant blanket rights to underlying Flickr photos. Before counting a mask, verify image ID and source image bytes/dimensions, annotation category/instance identity, polygon/RLE rasterization and orientation, and applicable image license; do not rescale an annotation to fit the acquired raster after seeing predictions.

**UI (30 positive, 10 negative).** Capture real, unmodified application windows from multiple source-disjoint application families, with 30 positive controls and 10 verified-negative target queries in total. Do not claim a ten-family requirement until a concrete, license-verified source roster is available; before freeze, publish a minimum family count and per-family case cap based on that roster. Exclude legacy Geany, Mousepad, AbiWord, and Calculator source families, and prevent overlap in build/theme/profile with earlier UI groups. Prefer mature, installable open-source desktop apps that expose an accessible component tree; pin the exact source roster privately before candidate freeze. For each positive, independently record target role/name/state and AT-SPI component extents in screen coordinates, then corroborate all four edges against the screenshot and inspect occlusion/clipping. The box is the complete visible control allocation, not just its glyph. For each negative, save a complete accessible inventory for the scoped visible control region and a same-state screenshot proving absence. Use private profiles and authored unsaved content; do not paste a colored annotation overlay into model input. Pin app version, build/package digest, toolkit, theme, fonts, scaling, window manager, capture dimensions, and each icon/theme/font license. AT-SPI's Component API explicitly supplies screen extents for visible/showing objects, making it a source oracle independent from model output: [AT-SPI Component.GetExtents](https://gnome.pages.gitlab.gnome.org/at-spi2-core/devel-docs/doc-org.a11y.atspi.Component.html).

**Maps/game maps (30 positive, 10 negative).** Capture 30 positive and 10 verified-negative map/game cases across multiple source-disjoint game/client/ruleset/renderer families. Do not claim a ten-family requirement until a concrete, license-verified source roster is available; before freeze, publish a minimum family count and per-family case cap based on that roster. Exclude every previously used or sealed game/client/ruleset/tileset family. Pin the exact source groups privately before candidate freeze. Run the actual application/client against private deterministic saves or scripted states with no network. For positives, retain a source trace/object ID, the map-to-window transform, renderer/asset provenance, and the actual visible sprite/marker alpha extent; independently verify that the recorded object is visible and unoccluded in the saved raster. For negatives, pair a same-state screenshot with the complete source-state inventory showing that the target type is absent from the visible map viewport. A save-file absence outside the screenshot viewport is insufficient. Keep full-map/world geometry separate from the visible object box. Verify the license for both code and every displayed graphics/tileset pack; the code license alone does not grant rights in arbitrary art. OpenTTD's official policy illustrates this separation: core game and OpenGFX use GPLv2, while add-on assets can carry other terms. [OpenTTD content creation/license guidance](https://www.openttd.org/content-creators). Do not scrape public tile servers for this cohort; if a future map plan uses OSM-derived tiles, obey the service policy and attribution, and do not bulk-download: [OSMF tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/).

Controlled group means an application/game family plus the pinned client/rendering/assets stack; multiple states in one group are correlated and stay together in uncertainty estimates. The photo acquisition plan requires at least 30 distinct source groups. Before selecting UI or map pixels, the curator must publish a concrete source roster and freeze the minimum family/group counts and per-family case caps that roster can support. Do not assume ten families: reject legacy Geany, Mousepad, AbiWord, Calculator, Freeciv, and other previously used families, and do not lower the fixed localization or mask gates to accommodate a thin roster. Require every stratum to cover target centers in each image quadrant, at least three target-size bands, and both uncluttered and cluttered or partially occluded (but still fully localizable) cases. Prefer independently verified official, source-aligned masks; commission double annotation and adjudication only for references that cannot be supplied from eligible official sources. There is no fixed 60-trace manual workload when verified official masks satisfy the quota. If the 30-reference mask quota or its independent provenance cannot be met by the audited source and annotation plan, stop before opening qualification rather than dropping or changing the mask gate. Stratify acquisition quotas before selection and publish aggregate counts only. No source group may cross old regression, active DEV, or qualification splits.

### Acquisition freeze and identities

The curator creates the following named, immutable cohort before any heldout producer run:

- Cohort ID: `localization-v03-qualification-120-v1`.
- Logical case IDs, never provider-facing: `photo-qual-001`…`photo-qual-040`, `ui-qual-001`…`ui-qual-040`, and `map-qual-001`…`map-qual-040`. Case IDs do not encode presence.
- Public, metadata-only commitment location: `docs/evidence/v03/qualification-120-v1-commitment.json` (cohort ID, 120-case count, 30/10 quotas, schema versions, exclusion-ledger digest, source-oracle code digests, and SHA-256 commitments to each private manifest/truth bundle; no case ID, target, source URL, image hash/path, or label).
- Curator-private files: separate input manifests, independent truth and mask index, source/license ledgers, raw oracle evidence, and pixel assets. They must live outside producer import roots and have read access restricted to the custodian/evaluator until the recipe freeze is signed.
- Producer freeze ID: `localization-v03-candidate-freeze-v1`; baseline freeze ID: `localization-v03-v02-final-v3-baseline-freeze-v1`; blind run IDs are distinct and include the cohort ID, profile ID, recipe digest, and phase `qualification`.

The private freeze binds exact input bytes and SHA-256, dimensions/orientation, case-to-stratum/source-group mapping, target semantics, box and mask bytes/hashes, independent evidence, licenses, source transforms, acquisition/exclusion audit, sampling seed, annotation/evaluator schema, every producer source file and dependency lock, prompts/policy, model/provider/API, native runtime/assets, deadlines/concurrency/threads, retry/fallback policy, physical-call/cost caps, and latency environment. Reconstruct and validate every identity before dispatch. Producer inputs contain only source pixels plus target/task text; no IDs, paths, source group, license, split, labels, boxes, masks, or oracle data. Record each producer output immutably; only after both complete runs close should the evaluator receive truth. The full evaluator output is then released with all 120 rows and artifact hashes; no missing row may be omitted.

Keep private heldout image and truth files out of Git, npm archives, implementation tasks, public logs, and tool outputs before freeze. A hash commitment is a seal/integrity check, not permission to expose paths or pixels. If the heldout source/policy or producer changes after freeze, invalidate that run for qualification and acquire a new cohort or issue a new, separately approved freeze before dispatch; never recalculate the gate.

## Low-cost DEV screens and iteration limits

The pinned 12-case triage manifest at `benchmarks/v03/pilot-inputs/{photo,ui,map}.json` uses only existing rows from the 40-case independent DEV set: four photos, four Geany UI captures, and four Freeciv map captures. It includes three positives and one negative per stratum. Producer-visible IDs are opaque (`pilot-v03-*`); source IDs and all truth are confined to `benchmarks/v03/evaluator-data/pilot-truth.json` and the evaluator module. The three input SHA-256 pins are photo `ee1d36d254f3aeddd9b9739143754dfbdc3409e155ab76334e10cf04e0bfb44b`, UI `5732d534990bfa98299a4e85f760acfa5319e0193e86bc182853558786cb0e9f`, and map `6886670e7f9529805196fe8502975f78c968ff612bfa3279c323cad215ba89c4`. The 8-case subset is the first two photo positives plus photo negative, first two UI positives plus UI negative, and first map positive plus map negative from those pinned arrays. It is five positives and three negatives under the corrected allocation above; its selected case IDs and 12-case outputs are reused, never redrawn. Source manifest hashes and selection were fixed before scoring. These screens are triage on existing DEV only. They do not consume heldout cases, do not qualify a candidate, and cannot replace the complete paired DEV40. Pair candidate and v0.2 baseline on identical rows. Retain every error and unknown. Use six DEV cells (photo/UI/map × positive/negative), fixed case IDs, and RNG seed `0x76303308`, recorded before inspecting candidate output. The 8-case allocation is 2 photo-positive, 1 photo-negative, 2 UI-positive, 1 UI-negative, 1 map-positive, and 1 map-negative (5 positives + 3 negatives = 8). The 12-case allocation is 3 positive and 1 negative from each stratum (9 positives + 3 negatives = 12). Draw uniformly without replacement within each cell, so each cell's per-case inclusion probability is its fixed quota divided by that cell's known DEV size. Precompute and publish both draws before scoring; if the 8-case gate passes, run the 12-case draw, reusing any exact case outputs shared between draws. Compute design-weighted success/failure totals with the fixed per-cell probabilities; show raw cell counts beside weighted estimates. Do not use the over-sampled negatives as an unweighted population accuracy number. Screens are paired and use the same draws for every candidate. Never redraw because of an unfavorable score.

Use the 8-case screen to catch hard failures in protocol, runtime, resource use, negative handling, and gross box quality before paying for a full run. Continue to the 12-case screen only if the 8-case run has no reproducible blocker and no apparent collapse versus the paired baseline. A negative false box is a serious diagnostic that stops the current attempt for investigation; it is not deleted or converted to an absence success. Then run a complete 40-case candidate/baseline DEV comparison only for hypotheses that pass the screen. Do not tune on 8/12 rows or claim screening significance.

Before each complete DEV40, preregister one hypothesis, its predicted failure mode, expected measurable change, exact code/prompt/profile digest, and the unchanged metric/gate. Allow at most two complete paired DEV40 attempts for that same hypothesis. If the second fails the predeclared development gate, preserve both reports and pivot to a materially different, named hypothesis. No third complete pass to search thresholds/prompts/seed points. A narrowly evidenced implementation defect is the exception: document a concrete reproduction and measurable consequence (for example, a deterministic wrong transform on a specified fixture), add a regression that fails before the patch and passes after it, and show it is independent of observed label outcomes. Repairing that defect permits one corrected full pass of the same recipe; it does not erase, replace, or relabel the earlier attempt. Accuracy disappointment, provider errors, or an unattractive confidence interval is not a software defect.

The development gate is fixed now: complete paired DEV40; zero false box/cutout cases among the ten explicit negatives; all 30 positive rows in the denominator; whole-object IoU/coverage metrics reported without filtering errors; and mask score with all reference masks, including no-prediction cases, retained. A candidate can proceed to final recipe freeze based on a large improvement in DEV localization, but only the new blind cohort can satisfy the 120-case qualification rule above. Do not select a different candidate after seeing the new qualification results.

## Run checklist and stopping rules

1. Audit and hash the complete old-source exclusion set. Build the private shortlist from source metadata only; screen licenses and source-group overlap before downloading pixels.
2. Acquire only the admitted cases, independently establish truth, run duplicate/orientation/geometry/license/oracle checks, and freeze the metadata-only commitment. Stop if any quota, negative oracle, independent mask-reference count, source-group count, license, or diversity target cannot be met; revise the plan before selection rather than weaken a gate.
3. Run existing-code offline checks and DEV-only screens. Complete no more than two full DEV attempts per hypothesis under the rule above. Freeze candidate, baseline, exact producer closure, masks/oracles, latency environment, and maximum physical/cost exposure before any qualification access.
4. Custodian executes baseline/candidate on all 120 cases under the frozen paired schedule, with receipts and physical dispatch accounting. Close both immutable runs before evaluator joins truth.
5. Evaluator validates commitments, all cases and all source identities, computes the fixed quality gates and source-group paired interval, then opens all results together. Report failure as failure with its exact receipt and preserve it. A future algorithm change needs new DEV selection and a newly acquired source-group-disjoint cohort; this cohort becomes regression-only after the reveal.

Do not invoke a paid provider, native model download, or broad corpus fetch as part of this design work. This document defines the study; acquisition, dispatch, qualification, and publication are separate authorized actions.
