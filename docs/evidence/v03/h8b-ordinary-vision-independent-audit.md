# H8b OpenAI GPT-6.1 Sol: independent audit

H8b completed all 12 frozen cases using 41 physical calls and 41 logical decisions. The independent audit verified all 41 physical receipts and raw decision traces, checked all 10 selected-mask artifacts, hashed 358 run/source files, and confirmed zero unknown charges and zero calls in flight. Known cost was USD 0.561926 against the corrected USD 5 ceiling. End-to-end replay time was 498,751 ms, including preparation and verification; it is not request latency.

On the nine positive cases, 8/9 had a candidate in the frozen native union. Presence admitted all nine actual-positive cases, and all eight union-available cases had an available nominee. Seven of nine ended in a selected candidate passing the unchanged same-mask gate: photo 3/3, UI 3/3, and maps 1/3. `pilot-v03-map-02` was a proposal-generation loss; `pilot-v03-map-01` had candidates and a nomination but ended `none-selected`.

The advancement gate fails. Two of three negatives, `pilot-v03-photo-04` and `pilot-v03-ui-04`, were false located outputs; the system said `present` and selected masks even though neither case had a matching candidate in the frozen union. `pilot-v03-map-04` was correctly marked absent. These are candidate-identity errors that the existing presence and `none` choices did not prevent. H8's rank-only outputs are not calibrated confidence. No tuning or replay was performed.

H8b therefore does not clear the frozen quality gate. Its seven positive passes match H8a's count and strata, and it reproduces the same two false-output cases. The result supports preparing a conditional H9 test with a separate, target-blind factual description of the selected mask and its visual context, followed by an explicit comparison to the original target and relation. This tests whether the chosen pixels actually depict the requested object rather than relying on the current target-primed presence/selection decisions. It cannot recover missing proposals, and it should remain an experiment under the same positive same-mask gate and zero-false-output requirement; the current results do not justify deployment or weakening the gate.
