# H8a OpenAI GPT-6 Luna: independent audit

H8a completed all 12 fixed cases with 41 physical calls, 41 logical decisions, no unknown charges, and USD 0.0359093 known cost. The independent audit verified all 41 persisted physical receipts and all 41 raw decision traces, hashed 358 run/source files, and checked all 11 selected source-sized masks against their frozen candidate masks.

The arm passed the positive portions of the advancement gate: same-mask available selections on all three photo positives, all three UI positives, and one of three maps. Across positives, 8/9 had a candidate in the frozen native union, all 8 were admitted by the presence stage, and all 8 had an available nominee; 7/9 ended in a selected candidate passing the same-mask gate. The generation loss was `pilot-v03-map-02`; the final-selection loss was `pilot-v03-map-03`.

The complete advancement gate **fails** because H8a produced two false outputs among the three negatives: `pilot-v03-photo-04` and `pilot-v03-ui-04`. `pilot-v03-map-04` was correctly marked absent. H8's ranking choices are rank-only; this audit does not treat them as calibrated confidence. The positive gain over H6 does not erase the two false outputs or meet the frozen gate.

The run took 580,562 ms end to end, including preparation and verification, not as request latency. The two false outputs arose at the presence stage, before candidate nomination or final mask selection. No tuning or replay was performed.

Three initial audit attempts exposed evaluator-only issues before scoring: relative source-closure paths were resolved from the evaluator worktree; the reused H6 receipt checker compared a persisted receipt with an H8 run record that adds `chat_parameters`; and the H8 physical route also carries those strict chat parameters. A supplemental capability-price comparison then used exact floating-point equality. Each issue is preserved in its attempt record; none changed the producer run or its receipts. The corrected audit also verified the capability JSON's SHA-256, OpenAI provider endpoint, image modality, structured-output support, reasoning parameter support, and frozen rate.
